Savanna Ecosystem Food Web Activity

Welcome to the ecosystem builder! Your task is to accurately map out the feeding relationships of 17 different African Savanna plants and animals based on real ecological data.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  1. Read the Data: Hover over any token for 0.75 seconds to view its Info Card, or go to the Organism Info Cards (Tab 2) to review them all at once.
  2. Place Your Organisms: Go to the Food Web Canvas (Tab 3). Click and drag the circular organism tokens from the left sidebar onto the white canvas workspace. You will need to place all 17 tokens to complete the web.
  3. Map the Energy Flow:
    • Click the red Draw Arrow Mode button in the top right corner so it turns green (ON).
    • Click on an organism that gets eaten (the source of energy). Its border will turn red.
    • Click on the organism that eats it (the consumer). An arrow will appear pointing from the prey to the predator, showing the transfer of energy.
  4. Adjust as Needed: Turn "Draw Arrow Mode" back OFF to move the tokens around the canvas. The arrows will stay attached and move with them. Arrange your web so it is easy to read (e.g., producers at the bottom, apex predators at the top).

Helpful Tips:

  • Remember that arrows point in the direction the energy flows (from the plant/prey into the mouth of the eater).
  • Hover over any token for a moment to check its info card without leaving the page!
  • Tokens can only be dragged onto the canvas once! If you make a massive mistake, you can use the Reset Canvas button to start over.
